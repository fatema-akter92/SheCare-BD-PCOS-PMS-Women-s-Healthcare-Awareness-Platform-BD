## Project Overview
**SheCare BD** is a dedicated, evidence-based digital healthcare and clinical consultation platform built specifically for adolescent girls and women across Bangladesh. It addresses two widespread, chronic, yet culturally stigmatized conditions:
1. **PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)** — an endocrine and metabolic disorder marked by irregular/absent menstruation, chronic anovulation, hyperandrogenism (facial/body hair, cystic acne, hair thinning), and cellular insulin resistance.
2. **PMS & PMDD (Premenstrual Syndrome / Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder)** — severe cyclical somatic cramps and disabling psychiatric symptoms (unprovoked crying spells, acute irritability, anxiety, and depression) occurring 7–10 days before menstruation.

In Bangladesh, **1 in every 5 young women** suffers from PCOS, yet more than **70% of cases remain undiagnosed** due to societal silence, fear, and cultural misconceptions regarding marriage and fertility. SheCare BD bridges this gap by combining an automated clinical screening engine, verified specialist doctor consultations at top offline Bangladeshi hospital chambers, discounted diagnostic lab panels, culturally attuned Desi lifestyle and nutritional directives, and 24/7 crisis support.

## 🚀 Core Features

### 1. Clinical Screening Engine (`/screening/`)
* **Rotterdam & ACOG Guidelines:** Evaluates cycle irregularities, facial hair (hirsutism), cystic acne, and insulin resistance markers.
* **Instant Risk Score:** Calculates PCOS risk (%) and PMS/PMDD severity with an official printable clinical referral report.

### 2. Doctors & Offline Chambers (`/doctors/`)
* **Verified Specialists:** Profiles of leading gynecologists, endocrinologists, and nutritionists from DMCH, BSMMU, and Evercare.
* **Chamber Booking:** Book in-person visits at top medical centers (Popular, Square, Ibn Sina, Evercare) or online video calls with digital token slips.

### 3. Diagnostic Tests & Lab Hub (`/diagnostic-tests/`)
* **Essential 5-Test Panel:** Pelvic USG, Testosterone, LH:FSH ratio, Fasting Insulin (HOMA-IR), and TSH/Vitamin D3.
* **20% Partner Discount:** Includes voucher code `SHECARE20` and pre-test fasting/sampling instructions.

### 4. Desi Nutrition & Myth Busters (`/awareness/`)
* **Bangladeshi Diet Guide:** Swapping white rice for Red Rice (*Lal Chal*), morning *Methi* water, and phase-based Seed Cycling.
* **Myth Busters:** Debunks 6 major societal stigmas regarding marriage, birth control pills, and fertility.
